## Workshop Awards

The ClinicalNLP 2027 workshop will recognize outstanding research contributions and shared task achievements through **six awards**, each carrying a cash prize of USD $1,000. 
## Paper Awards

The following awards recognize papers demonstrating scientific excellence, originality, innovation, and potential impact.

- **Best Workshop Paper Award ($1,000):** Recognizes the most outstanding paper presented at the workshop.

- **Best Student Paper Award ($1,000):** Honors an exceptional student-led paper demonstrating scientific excellence and research impact.

- **Best Shared Task Paper Award ($1,000):** Recognizes an outstanding shared task paper, emphasizing originality, innovative methodologies, and insightful analysis.

## Shared Task Awards

The workshop will recognize the top-performing teams in each of the three shared task subtasks:

- **MEDIQA-RADEVAL Shared Task Winner ($1,000)**
- **MEDIQA-MULTIREFEVAL Shared Task Winner - English Subtask ($1,000)**
- **MEDIQA-MULTIREFEVAL Shared Task Winner - Chinese Subtask ($1,000)**

## Award Announcements

All award recipients will be announced during the ClinicalNLP 2027 workshop.
